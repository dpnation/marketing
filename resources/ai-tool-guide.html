Choosing the Right AI Tool

A quick-reference guide for marketing team members deciding between ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity — matched to the task, not personal preference.

Tool Overview

What each tool is built for and where it tends to shine.

ChatGPT: All-purpose productivity and agent-based automation. Well suited to brainstorming, coding reviews, and recurring research tasks, with generous usage limits.
Gemini: Large-scale document analysis and Google Workspace integration. Strong at multimodal content (video and image analysis) and visual dashboards, backed by a 2M-token context window.
Claude: Strategic thinking and voice-matched content creation. A strong fit for maintaining a consistent tone, building specialized workflows, and serving as a thought partner for complex decisions.
Perplexity: Real-time research with verified sources. The go-to for fact-checking, trend monitoring, and gathering current information with trustworthy citations.

3-Question Framework: Choosing the Right Tool

Ask these three questions before prompting any AI tool, in order, to land on the right one for the task.

1. Does this need real-time or current information?

If yes, use Perplexity.

2. Does this require strategic thinking, nuanced writing, or complex workflows involving external tool connections?

If yes, use Claude.

3. Is this a general task, exploration, or does it need AI to autonomously browse the web, navigate sites, or execute tasks?

If yes, use ChatGPT or Gemini. For work involving Google tools or large files, Gemini is the stronger pick.

What Each Tool Isn't Good For

Understanding limitations matters as much as knowing strengths. Proceed with caution in these areas.

ChatGPT

Avoid for real-time information needs — it will confidently generate outdated statistics. Also weaker for high-accuracy data analysis compared to other models.

Gemini

Excellent for prototyping but inconsistent with detailed coding. Integration support outside Google's ecosystem is limited, making it a weaker fit for complex third-party workflows.

Claude

Strict usage limits, especially weekly caps, make it unsuitable for high-volume tasks. Pre-process large datasets with other tools before bringing refined questions to Claude.

Perplexity

Laser-focused on search — not built for general productivity, creative work, or workflow automation. Even across models, it stays optimized for search-based responses.
