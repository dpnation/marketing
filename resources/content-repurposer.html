Content Repurposer

Takes one piece of long-form Applause content and produces a full pack of channel-ready outputs — one input, multiple deliverables, no copy-pasting between tools.

What It Does

Blog post → LinkedIn post. Webinar → follow-up email. Whitepaper → nurture copy. This eliminates the manual rewrite step. Paste or upload long-form content — blog post, whitepaper, webinar/podcast transcript, case study, press release, or product one-pager — and it auto-detects the content type, then generates a full Content Pack rewritten in Applause voice.

LinkedIn post150–250 words, all content types
Executive summary100–150 words, all content types

Slide outline5–8 slides, all content types

How It Works

Four steps — it shows its work before generating anything.

1. Intake

Auto-detects content type, primary topic, Applause solution area, and audience signals. Shows a short interpretation — content type, topic, solution area, audience, planned outputs — and waits for "go" before generating.

2. Output selection

Accept the defaults, or say "just the LinkedIn post and email," "full pack," "add the analyst summary," or "skip the slide outline."

3. Generation

Reads the source, extracts the strongest proof points and quotes, and rewrites — not summarizes verbatim — in Applause voice, tailored per channel. LinkedIn, email, and an exec summary are treated as genuinely different jobs, not one draft truncated five ways.

4. Package and present

Three or more outputs get packaged into a single .docx Content Pack. One or two outputs deliver inline. Either way, it closes with what was generated, any [needs verification] fields, and a suggested next step.

Brand Guardrails Built In

Applied to every output, every run — non-negotiable regardless of channel.

Canonical stat

"1.5M+ independent experts and end users" — never "1M+" or another figure.

No product names

AI capabilities referenced generically, never by internal product name.

Crowdtesting stays supporting

One word, lowercase, used sparingly — real-world testing or the specific solution area leads instead.

No fabrication

Stats, quotes, or customer names not in the source get flagged [needs verification], never invented.
