Buyer Evaluation — Skill Pairing

Two readers, one piece of content: the human buyer's reaction, and the AI research assistant increasingly doing that reading on the buyer's behalf.

Run these two skills on the same asset — a landing page, a one-pager, a deck — to check it clears the bar twice. persona-council tells you if a human buyer keeps reading. ai-agent-persona tells you if an AI assistant researching on that buyer's behalf finds enough evidence to keep you on the shortlist. Neither produces the other; they run independently and land on the same question.

How to read this

This isn't a workflow with a fixed order — run persona-council and ai-agent-persona in either sequence, or in parallel. Each evaluates the same asset from a different vantage point, and both feed the same verdict: is there enough here to keep a deal alive.

persona-council answers the question a marketer has always had to answer: would our buyer actually read this and believe it? ai-agent-persona answers the newer one: if that buyer delegates the research to an AI assistant, does the assistant find enough to recommend us at all?

Distinct from the AEO skill family — AEO tracks whether AI search engines cite and crawl our content. This pairing tracks whether an AI shopping assistant, already evaluating us on a buyer's behalf, has enough to keep us in the running.
