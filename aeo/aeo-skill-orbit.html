One program, six skills. Three audit tracks run every quarter and feed a shared quality gate; a fourth audit runs ad hoc. Two more sit downstream, turning audit findings into the actual fix or published page. This map shows how they relate — not a workflow to follow step by step.

How to read this

The inner ring is the operating rhythm: three tracks run every quarter, each answering a different question about the same set of pages — content-audit checks the copy, technical-audit checks the plumbing, visibility-audit checks brand-level standing across AI platforms.

The outer ring is lower priority — offsite-signals runs whenever a source log is available, not on a fixed schedule, and looks at the category rather than a specific page.

applause-aeo-evaluator doesn't sit on either ring. It's a QA pass that reviews a finished report from any track before it goes to leadership — run it on demand, not automatically.

Two more skills sit downstream of the ring, not on it: schema-markup turns a technical audit's schema gap into the actual fix, and applause-aeo-comparison-pages turns a visibility audit's citation gap into a published "vs." or "alternatives" page. Both consume a track's findings — they don't audit anything themselves.
