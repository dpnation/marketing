Q3 2026 baseline complete and confirmed — all 24 core pages audited on applause-aeo-technical-audit (Aug 4, 2026), then re-verified against the live rendered DOM via Claude in Chrome to resolve every schema and structure row that the fetch tool could only mark Needs Verification. Two sitewide gaps now confirmed on all 24 pages: no page has a real <main> landmark, and no page carries any schema beyond WebSite/WebPage — no FAQPage, Article, HowTo, Product/Service, or BreadcrumbList anywhere, and Organization schema is missing even on About Applause. Four pages are now confirmed Blocked: Contact (Marketo form renders with zero input fields even after full JS execution), Jobs (no job listings or ATS embed anywhere in the rendered DOM), STLC (unrelated "neobroker" fintech content confirmed mixed into the live page), and Digital Quality Solutions (canonical tag confirmed pointing to /our-services/ instead of itself). Watch Demo is also confirmed Blocked — no video, iframe, or form element exists anywhere on the page. QA vs QC confirmed 0 <table>/<dl> elements — its core comparison isn't real markup. Most blog posts confirmed skipping straight from H1 to H4/H5, and the majority of images sitewide (roughly 60–90% per page) confirmed missing alt text.
