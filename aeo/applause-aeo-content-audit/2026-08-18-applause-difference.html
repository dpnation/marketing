Flags

  • Preset overlap: this page could also be scored as Homepage; Real-world/community testing was chosen as the stronger, more specific fit given the page's actual emphasis on crowdtesting and the global tester community.
  • Near-miss terms: "crowdtesting" and "global testing community" both appear but don't clear the close-variant bar against their approved-list counterparts ("crowdsourced QA," "global tester community") — worth a conversation about whether the keyword list should track the phrasing Applause actually uses.
  • Mismatched alt text: the Audi case-study logo image reads "Étude de cas Oomnitza" — wrong company, wrong language. A real Extractability defect, not a fetch-tool artifact.
  • No visible date, byline, or named owner anywhere on the page — the same structural gap found on every Applause solution/brand page audited this project (Payments, Digital Quality Solutions), driving the 3/20 Attribution & Freshness score here.
  • Page fetched live and complete; several images render with empty src in the fetch (recurring Divi pattern across this project).
